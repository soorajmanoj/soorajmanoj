### Research

**[Bias in AI-Generated Travel Narratives: Evaluating LLMs in Countering Misconceptions](https://github.com/soorajmanoj/Bias-in-AI-Generated-Travel-Narratives)** — DSCI 602 final paper, RIT
Co-authored with Anshuman Mohapatra, Bhaskar Sreenivas Pavan Akkena, and advisors Ashique Khudabukhsh and Travis Desell. A 5-layer pipeline collecting ~600K YouTube travel comments, generating counterspeech across four LLMs (LLaMA32, Gemma2, GPT2, Sarvam), and comparing human toxicity ratings against Detoxify scores — finding that automated toxicity classifiers systematically underestimate human-perceived harm and produce unstable model safety rankings.
